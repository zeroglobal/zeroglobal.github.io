SPL Cash Flow Automation

Privacy policy · Last updated September 28, 2026

SPL Cash Flow Automation is a private, single-user tool operated by its owner (wackford.link@gmail.com). It is not offered to the public.

What it does

Once a week it reads bank export files from a folder in the owner's own Google Drive, builds a cash flow workbook and summary, and saves them back to the same Drive.

Privacy policy

The app accesses only the owner's Google Drive, using the owner's own authorization. It does not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data or Google user data to third parties. Data read from Google Drive is used solely to produce the owner's own reports and is not used for advertising or any other purpose. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
